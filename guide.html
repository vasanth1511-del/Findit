The idea behind FindIt

A small tool for a real problem.

Students often leave home and realize something important is still on the desk. FindIt helps make that less likely.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Small things are easy to forget

In a hurry, it is easy to leave behind an ID card, charger, assignment, or water bottle. Remembering every item takes extra effort.

02 / THE SOLUTION

A checklist before leaving

FindIt keeps everyday essentials in one place. Check each thing off as you pack and see at a glance what is still missing.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Simple browser technology

HTML builds the pages, CSS makes them responsive, and JavaScript adds items, handles checkboxes, calculates progress, and updates the ready status.

04 / REMEMBERING YOUR LIST

Saved on this device

The browser's local storage keeps checklist items and attached photos between visits. FindIt works without an account or a server.

Show it in a presentation: “FindIt helps students avoid forgetting everyday items. Students can build a personal list, attach photos to reminders, and check things off before leaving. The browser saves the list, so it is ready the next time they open the app.”
